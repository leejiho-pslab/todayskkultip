ITZY and NMIXX — two of the biggest JYP girl groups in K-pop right now — are easy to confuse when you're just starting out, but once you know what makes each group tick, you'll wonder how you ever mixed them up. Whether you've been sent down a rabbit hole by a TikTok clip or stumbled across a music show performance, this guide to **ITZY vs NMIXX** will give you everything you need to choose your entry point, pick a bias, and join one of the most passionate fanbases in K-pop.

---

## Who Are ITZY? The Confident Girl-Gang You Need to Know

**ITZY debuted on February 12, 2019, under JYP Entertainment**, and they hit the ground running with "DALLA DALLA" — a song that basically told the world: *we don't care what you think of us*. That self-love, unapologetic confidence became their signature, and five years on, it still holds. The group has five members: **Yeji, Lia, Ryujin, Chaeryeon, and Yuna**.

What makes ITZY stand out is their consistency of message. Every comeback — from "WANNABE" to "SNEAKERS" to their 2025 releases — doubles down on the theme of being yourself without apology. Their sound lives in the pop-dance lane, often with hip-hop edges and punchy hooks that get stuck in your head immediately. If you've ever watched a live stage and thought *these girls look like they're having the time of their lives*, that's the ITZY effect.

Their fandom is called **MIDZY**, and it's one of the more welcoming entry-level fandoms in K-pop — great for newcomers. As of August 2026, ITZY has amassed over **18 million followers on Instagram** ([@itzy.all.in.us](https://www.instagram.com/itzy.all.in.us/)), making them one of the most-followed K-pop groups globally. You can find their official content and discography on [JYP Entertainment's official site](https://www.jype.com).

**Best first song:** "WANNABE" (2020) — three minutes of pure hype.
**Best for:** Fans of Charli XCX, Blackpink's louder side, or anyone who likes a confident main character.

---

## Who Are NMIXX? JYP's Experimental Genre-Benders Explained

**NMIXX debuted on February 22, 2022**, and if ITZY's debut was a confident punch, NMIXX's debut was a philosophical question. Their first single "O.O" dropped jaws — and not always in a good way, at first. The song deliberately smashed multiple genres together mid-track, a structural choice JYP called **"MIXX POP."** Some fans got it immediately; others needed a few listens. Either way, it sparked a conversation, which is exactly what JYP wanted.

The group currently has six members: **Lily, Haewon, Sullyoon, Bae, Jiwoo, and Kyujin** (former member Jinni departed in December 2022). Their vocal ability is widely regarded as among the best of any fourth-generation girl group — Lily and Sullyoon in particular regularly trend after live stages. Their performances are technically demanding, often combining intricate choreography with key changes that would challenge even seasoned vocalists.

By 2025–2026, NMIXX had hit a real stride. Comebacks like "COOL (Your Rainbow)" and their later title tracks showed a group growing into their identity — still experimental, but more confident in how they deliver it. Their fandom is called **NSWER** (pronounced "answer"). As of August 2026, you can follow them on [NMIXX's official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/nmixx_official/) and access full discography details through [JYP Entertainment](https://www.jype.com).

**Best first song:** "DICE" (2022) — cleaner structure than "O.O" but still shows their genre-bending DNA.
**Best for:** Fans of complex pop production, strong vocalists, or groups like f(x) or Red Velvet's weirder side.

## ITZY vs NMIXX: A Side-by-Side Comparison

Here's where things get practical. If you're a new fan trying to decide where to dive in, this table should help you orient yourself fast.

| Feature | ITZY | NMIXX |
|---|---|---|
| **Debut year** | 2019 | 2022 |
| **Members** | 5 (Yeji, Lia, Ryujin, Chaeryeon, Yuna) | 6 (Lily, Haewon, Sullyoon, Bae, Jiwoo, Kyujin) |

**Bottom line: ITZY is the easier on-ramp; NMIXX rewards patience and repeated listening.** That said, plenty of fans — myself included, after covering K-pop for years — end up stanning both, because they scratch very different itches.

## How to Actually Get Into Each Group (A Step-by-Step Guide)

Starting a new K-pop fandom can feel overwhelming, so here's a simple approach that actually works:

1. **Pick your entry track.** For ITZY: "WANNABE" or "DALLA DALLA." For NMIXX: "DICE" or "Cool (Your Rainbow)." Don't start with the most recent comeback — build context first.
2. **Watch a live performance, not just the MV.** Both groups shine on stage. Search for ITZY or NMIXX on [Mnet's M COUNTDOWN YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@MnetMcountdown) — it's free and the audio/video quality is excellent.
3. **Learn two or three members' names first.** Trying to learn all members at once leads to burnout. Start with whoever catches your eye in a performance.
4. **Join the fandom community.** Reddit's [r/kpop](https://www.reddit.com/r/kpop/) is a solid, relatively welcoming space for newcomers. Both MIDZY and NSWER have active Twitter/X communities.
5. **Follow the official fan cafe or Weverse.** Both ITZY and NMIXX are active on Weverse, where members post updates and interact with fans directly. It's free to join.
6. **Stream an album front-to-back.** B-sides often reveal what a group is really about. ITZY's *CRAZY IN LOVE* and NMIXX's *expergo* are great full-listen projects.

## Concerts, Fan Meets & Seeing Them Live in 2026

One of the best things about stanning an active JYP group right now is that **both ITZY and NMIXX are touring actively as of 2026**. ITZY, having been around since 2019, has a more established concert circuit and has done multiple world tours. NMIXX held their first solo concert in 2023 and has since expanded to international stops.

For international fans planning a trip to Seoul, catching a music show taping is one of the most affordable and immersive ways to see your favorite group live. Shows like *M COUNTDOWN*, *Music Bank*, and *Inkigayo* are free to attend (with advance application), and JYP groups frequently appear on all three. Check the [Korea Tourism Organization's official site (VisitKorea)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr) for updated guides on music show audiences and fan event access as of August 2026 — policies and application windows change seasonally, so always verify before booking flights.

For ticketed events, **ITZY's MIDZY fan meetings and NMIXX's NSWER fan cons** sell out fast — typically within minutes of going on sale. Set up alerts on Weverse and follow official JYP social channels for presale codes. If you're buying from a resale market, stick to verified platforms and be cautious of unofficial sellers.

## Which Group Should YOU Stan? An Honest Take

Here's my honest take after following both groups closely: **your personality type matters more than the music genre**.

If you like things that click immediately — a hook that hits on first listen, a stage presence that reads across a room — **ITZY is your group**. Ryujin's rap energy, Chaeryeon's dancing, Yuna's visuals — there's someone for everyone and the entry barrier is low.

If you're the type who appreciates craft, enjoys dissecting production choices, and likes music that reveals new layers on each relisten — **NMIXX will reward you endlessly**. Sullyoon's vocals alone have converted many skeptics. The group's trajectory from "divisive debut" to "respected artistry" is one of the most compelling stories in 4th-gen K-pop.

And if you genuinely can't choose? Welcome to the JYP Extended Universe. Plenty of fans support both — the groups have different enough aesthetics that there's no real conflict. Think of it like liking both coffee and tea. You just get more options.
