If you've recently fallen into the TWICE rabbit hole and found yourself staring at a wall of album covers wondering where on earth to begin, the **TWICE discography** is both a joy and a challenge — because there is *a lot* of it. As someone who's been covering K-pop in Seoul since the JYP Entertainment building on Cheongdam-dong was still the coolest landmark on the block, I want to give you the clearest, most honest map of TWICE's **albums and eras** I can. By the end of this piece, you'll know what each album era sounds like, what it meant for the group, and in what order to experience it all.

Let's get into it.

---

## Era 1 (2015–2016): The Debut Burst — "The Story Begins" to "TWICEcoaster"

**TWICE debuted with a sound so instantly catchy it almost felt unfair to every other group launching that year.** The nine-member group — Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu — was formed through the Mnet survival show *SIXTEEN* and signed to [JYP Entertainment](https://www.jypeartiststwice.com/), one of Korea's "Big Four" agencies. Their October 2015 debut mini-album ***The Story Begins*** introduced "Like OOH-AHH," a track that blended a slightly edgy zombie concept with an irresistibly bouncy hook. It charted immediately and set a template: give TWICE a chorus, and it will live in your head for weeks.

What followed in 2016 was a release pace that felt almost reckless. ***PAGE TWO*** (April 2016) brought "CHEER UP," which won Song of the Year at multiple major award shows. ***TWICEcoaster: Lane 1*** (October 2016) delivered "TT," arguably their most iconic song of the early era — the hand gesture alone became a nationwide trend in South Korea (as of late 2016, the "TT" pose was everywhere from school hallways to news broadcasts). These two mini-albums defined what critics called **"the cute era"**: bright colors, playful choreography, and hooks engineered for maximum replay.

| Album | Release | Lead Single | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| *The Story Begins* | Oct 2015 | Like OOH-AHH | Edgy-cute debut |
| *PAGE TWO* | Apr 2016 | CHEER UP | Cheerleader pop |
| *TWICEcoaster: Lane 1* | Oct 2016 | TT | Costume concept pop |

---

## Era 2 (2017–2018): Going Bigger — "Signal" to "YES or YES"

**2017–2018 was when TWICE proved the debut wasn't a fluke — they scaled up in ambition, sound, and fanbase.** ***Signal*** (May 2017) divided fans at first (the production was deliberately quirky and slightly alien-sounding), but "Signal" grew on everyone fast. ***Likey*** from ***Twicetagram*** (October 2017) went full Instagram-aesthetic and became one of their most streamed tracks. ***What is Love?*** (April 2018) leaned into a cinematic, movie-homage concept that showed a new visual maturity.

Their first full-length Korean album, ***twicetagram***, also launched their Japanese parallel discography into high gear. By this point TWICE was simultaneously charting on [Oricon](https://www.oricon.co.jp/) in Japan and Gaon in Korea — a dual-market dominance very few groups pull off. ***YES or YES*** (November 2018) closed out this period with a bolder, more confident sound. This era is often called the **"growth era"** — the group was transitioning from "cute rookies" to a genuine long-term act.

> **Pro tip for new fans:** If you only have time for one album from this era, make it *What is Love?* — the concept, the choreo, and the b-sides are all excellent entry points.

---

## Era 3 (2019–2020): The Cool Pivot — "FANCY YOU" to "Eyes Wide Open"

**"FANCY" (April 2019) is the moment most fans point to as TWICE's official "glow-up" — the sound got sleeker, the visuals got sharper, and the group seemed to step fully into their own.** The ***FANCY YOU*** mini-album dropped the hyper-cute palette for jewel tones and a more sophisticated synth-pop production. It was genuinely exciting to watch in real time. ***Feel Special*** (September 2019) followed with an emotionally resonant ballad-pop lead single that Jihyo co-wrote, and the music video's message about self-worth made it one of the most-shared TWICE videos of the year.

Their second full Korean studio album, ***Eyes Wide Open*** (October 2020), landed during the pandemic and pushed further into funk, R&B, and dance-pop territory. Tracks like "I CAN'T STOP ME" and "BEHIND THE MASK" showed a group that was actively refusing to be boxed in. **As of August 2026, "I CAN'T STOP ME" remains one of their top-streamed tracks globally on Spotify.** This era matters because it's where casual listeners became committed ONCEs (the official TWICE fandom name).

| Album | Release | Vibe Shift |
|---|---|---|
| *FANCY YOU* | Apr 2019 | Glam synth-pop |
| *Feel Special* | Sep 2019 | Emotional empowerment |
| *More & More* | Jun 2020 | Mystical, darker aesthetic |
| *Eyes Wide Open* | Oct 2020 | Full R&B/funk studio album |

---

## Era 4 (2021–2022): The Global Push — English Singles and "Formula of Love"

**TWICE became the first K-pop girl group to sell out the SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles — and this era is exactly how they got there.** 2021 opened with their first English-language single, "Alcohol-Free" was actually in Korean, but ***"The Feels"*** (October 2021) was their proper English-language debut single, written for a Western pop crossover. It charted on the Billboard Hot 100, a milestone covered by [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/k-pop/twice-the-feels-billboard-hot-100-1235009739/) at the time.

***Formula of Love: O+T=<3*** (November 2021), their third full Korean album, is widely considered one of their best artistic statements — a concept album structured around a fictional "love formula" with cohesive visual and sonic theming. If you want to understand why the critical consensus on TWICE shifted from "commercial pop act" to "genuine artists," this is the album to put on. ***BETWEEN 1&2*** (August 2022) continued the streak with the stunning retro-pop lead "Talk that Talk."

---

## Era 5 (2023–2024): Maturity and Member Solo Work — "READY TO BE" and Beyond

**By 2023, TWICE was navigating the natural tension between group releases and members' growing individual careers — and they handled it with remarkable grace.** ***READY TO BE*** (March 2023) delivered "SET ME FREE," a powerful pop-dance track where the group's confidence was unmistakable. The accompanying world tour, *TWICE 5TH WORLD TOUR 'READY TO BE'*, brought them to arenas across North America, Europe, and Asia.

During this period, members including Nayeon, Jihyo, and Chaeyoung released solo projects. Nayeon's solo mini-album ***IM NAYEON*** (2022) had already broken ground; Jihyo's ***ZONE*** (2023) went in a more mature, introspective direction. For new fans, this is a good moment to branch into solo discographies once you've got the group albums down. Check the official [TWICE website](https://www.jypeartiststwice.com/) for the most current solo and group release schedules, as lineups and release dates can shift.

---

## Era 6 (2025–2026): Where TWICE Stands Right Now

**As of August 2026, TWICE is in arguably the most creatively free period of their career, with all nine members having re-signed with JYP Entertainment and a new album cycle underway.** The re-signing news in 2023 was a massive deal — historically, many fourth-generation and third-generation groups splinter after initial contracts end. TWICE staying together as a nine-piece sent a clear message to the industry.

Their 2025 releases pushed further into a cinematic, genre-fluid sound. New fans jumping in right now have the rare luxury of experiencing a group at peak artistic confidence *and* having a decade of back catalogue to explore. For the latest release dates and tour announcements, always check the official [JYP Entertainment TWICE page](https://www.jypeartiststwice.com/) — schedules and ticket sales change frequently.

## How to Start the TWICE Discography: A Practical Listening Roadmap

Not sure where to begin? Here's the order I'd recommend for a new fan who wants to understand the full TWICE arc without getting overwhelmed:

1. **Start with "TT" and "CHEER UP"** — understand where they came from and why Korea went wild.
2. **Listen to *FANCY YOU*** — hear the moment the sound shifted.
3. **Put on *Formula of Love* front to back** — this is their artistic peak, and it works as a standalone album experience.
4. **Watch the *Feel Special* music video** — the emotional core of the ONCE fandom is right here.
5. **Explore "I CAN'T STOP ME" and *Eyes Wide Open*** — get into the R&B/funk period.
6. **Catch up with 2025–2026 releases** — you're now current.

## Key Takeaways: The TWICE Discography at a Glance

- **Debut era (2015–2016):** Cute-concept pop dominance; "TT" and "CHEER UP" are the gateway drugs.
- **Growth era (2017–2018):** Dual Korean/Japanese market dominance; "What is Love?" is the standout.
- **Cool pivot (2019–2020):** *FANCY YOU* and *Eyes Wide Open* mark the artistic maturation.
- **Global push (2021–2022):** English singles, Billboard charting, *Formula of Love* as a career-best.
- **Solo era (2023–2024):** Group + individual projects; *READY TO BE* world tour proves their live power.
- **2025–2026:** All nine members together, new music, creative peak. A great time to become an ONCE.
